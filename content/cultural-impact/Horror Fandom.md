### Horror Fandom

Horror fandom is a large community built around people's shared interest in horror movies, characters, monsters and the culture surrounding the genre. Horror fans engage with movies in different ways from watching and discussing films to attending conventions.

#### Fan Communities

Horror fans connect through both in-person and online communities. Social media has made it easier for horror fans to participate in fandom regardless of where they live.

#### Horror Collectibles
Collecting is another important part of horror fandom. Fans collect posters, DVDs, figures, masks, clothing and other memorabilia.

#### Horror Fandom and Popular Culture

The relationship between horror fandom and popular culture is visible through recognizable characters and imagery.
