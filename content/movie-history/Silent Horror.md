### Silent Horror

Silent horror generally refers to early horror cinema from the silent film era (late 1890s to late 1920s) that relied on visuals, shadows, and acting rather than spoken dialogue or synchronized sound.

##### Visual Storytelling

Without spoken dialogue, silent horror depended heavily on what audiences could see. Directors used lighting, shadows, camera angles to communicate fear.

Shadows were especially important because a character could be frightened by something that the audience could not see.

#### Important Silent Horror Films

- Le Manoir de Diable (1896) - Georges Méliès 
- The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari (1920) - Robert Wiene
- Nosferatu (1922) - F.W. Murnau 

These films explored different forms of horror.
