## Horror Movies in Popular Culture

Horror has been an evolving force in popular culture for over a century. What started as a genre focused on frightening audiences has grown into a major influence on movies, television, video games, music and social media.

#### Horror in Movies and Television

Horror television has helped bring the genre to larger audiences through shows that combine horror with mystery, drama, comedy and science fiction.

#### Horror and Halloween

Horror and Halloween have become closely connected in American popular culture. Halloween provides the opportunity for people to experience horror through costumes, decorations, haunted houses and scary movies.

#### Cultural Impact

Horror's influence can be seen beyond the movie theater. Its characters, imagery, stories and traditions have become part of everyday popular culture.
