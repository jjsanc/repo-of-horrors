### Iconic Horror Villains

Horror movies have created some of the most recognizable villains in film history. These characters often become symbols of the fears, themes, and storytelling styles associated with horror.

#### Michael Myers

Michael Myers is the masked killer from the Halloween franchise. He first appeared in John Carpenter's Halloween (1978) and quickly became one of the most recognizable figures in the slasher genre.

Michael is known for his blank white mask, dark clothing, silence, and relentless pursuit of his victims. His lack of dialogue is an important part of what makes the character frightening.

### Other Iconic Horror Villains
#### Freddy Krueger

Freddy Krueger is the main villain of A Nightmare on Elm Street. He attacks victims through their dreams, making sleep itself dangerous.

#### Ghostface

Ghostface is the masked killer identity used throughout the Scream franchise. Unlike characters such as Michael Myers, Ghostface is not one specific person.
