# About

Welcome to my horror movie website! I created this site to explore one of my favorite movie genres and learn more about what makes horror so interesting.

#### What Is This Website About?

When most people think about horror, they immediately think about scary movies, monsters, or jump scares. But there is more to the genre that that.

This website looks at how horror movies have developed over time, the movies and characters that have become iconic and the different techniques filmmakers use to scare their audience.

#### Who Is This For

This website is for anyone who enjoys horror movies or wants to learn more about them. You don't have to be a huge horror fan to explore the site.

#### Why Horror?

I chose horror because it is a genre that has been around for a long time but continues to change. 

For me, horror is interesting because it can be scary and entertaining, but it can also tell stories about people's fears and things that make us uncomfortable.
