### Universal Monsters Era

The Universal Monsters era is a foundational period of classic Hollywood horror spanning roughly from 1923 to 1960, produced primarily by Universal Pictures.

### The Golden Age of Universal Horror

#### Frankenstein

One of Universal's biggest successes came with Frankenstein (1931). The film became famous for its gothic atmosphere and laboratory scenes. Frankenstein's monster became one of the defining images of classic horror.

#### The Mummy

The 1932 classic horror film The Mummy follows an ancient Egyptian priest who is accidentally brought back to life by a modern archaeological expedition and searches for the reincarnation of his forbidden lover.

#### The Wolf Man

The 1941 classic horror film The Wolf Man follows Larry Talbot (Lon Chaney Jr.) as he returns to his ancestral home in Wales and becomes cursed with lycanthropy after fighting off a werewolf.

### Legacy of the Universal Monsters

The Universal monsters remain an important part of horror movie history because they helped transform monsters into characters with their own stories and struggles.
