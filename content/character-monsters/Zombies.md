## Zombies

Zombies are one of the most recognizable creatures in horror. They are usually portrayed as reanimated corpses that attack and consume living people.

#### Zombies in Horror Films

One of the most important movies in zombie cinema came with George A. Romero's Night of the *Living Dead* (1968).

The film also demonstrated how horror could address social concerns. Zombie movies became associated not only with scares and violence but also with themes such as fear, isolation and survival.

#### Dawn of the Dead

Romero's Dawn of the Dead expanded the zombie concept and used a shopping mall as an important setting. 
#### 28 Days Later (2002)

28 Days Later presented infected humans who moved extremely quickly, helping popularize a different interpretation of the zombie-like threat.
