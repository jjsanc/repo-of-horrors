### Practical Effects

Practical effects in horror movies rely on physical, tangible methods on set to create gore, monsters, and suspense without relying solely on computers.

#### Core Practical Techniques

- Special Effects Makeup & Prosthetics: Artists use materials like silicone, gelatin, and tissue paper with glue to build realistic wounds, rotting flesh
- Fake Blood: Standard recipes mix corn syrup, red food coloring, and a dash of chocolate syrup or blue paint to get the right dark, viscous look for wounds.
- Specialized Props: Stabbing and slashing scenes use rubber knives for safety during fast movement, retractable blades that shorten upon impact
