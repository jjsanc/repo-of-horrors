### Lighting and Color

Horror filmmakers use low-key lighting and high-contrast color palettes to manipulate shadows, conceal threats, and evoke deep psychological unease.

#### Lighting Techniques

- Underlighting: Placing a light source below a character's face casts upward shadows, distorting normal facial features into a menacing appearance.
- Silhouettes & Backlighting: Positioning a harsh light behind a subject turns them into a mysterious, threatening shape while hiding their identity.
- Flickering & Dynamic Effects: Using faulty bulbs, lightning, or simulated television screen flicker creates erratic shadows that keep viewers on edge
