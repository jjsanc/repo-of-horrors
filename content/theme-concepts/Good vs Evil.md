### Good vs. Evil

A lot of horror movies have characters who are trying to protect themselves or others from something dangerous. The evil side could be a killer, monster, ghost, demon or even a person who chooses to hurt others. This conflict gives the character something to fight against.

### Heroes and Villains

Horror movies often have a clear difference between the characters trying to survive and the ones causing danger. The main character usually has to find a way to defeat evil or escape from it.

### Why Good vs. Evil Works in Horror

For me, this theme makes horror more interesting because it gives the character a reason to keep fighting even when things seem hopeless. It also makes me wonder how far someone would go to protect themselves or the people they care about. 

#### Example: The Exorcist

The Exorcist is a good example of the conflict between good and evil.
