## Camera Angles

Camera angles can change how the audience understands a scene or character.

- Dutch Angle (Tilted Angle): Tilts the camera horizon off-center to create immediate visual imbalance, disorientation, and a nightmare feel.
- Point-of-View (POV) Shot: Places the audience directly behind the eyes of the killer or the victim to build intense, personal identification or dread.
- Extreme Close-Ups: Zooms closely onto eyes, a screaming mouth, or an object trigger claustrophobia and raw emotional panic.

Horror movies use specific camera angles and framing choices to create tension, psychological unease, and fear
