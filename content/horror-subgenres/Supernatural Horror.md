## Supernatural Horror

Supernatural horror is a horror subgenre that focuses on frightening events caused by forces that cannot be explained by the normal laws of nature. The forces can include ghosts, demons, spirits, possessions and other paranormal entities.

#### Common Elements of Supernatural Horror

#### Demons and Possession

Some supernatural horror focuses on demons and evil entities that take control of people
Examples
- the Exorcist (1973)
- The Conjuring (2013)
- The Exorcism of Emily Rose (2005)

The Unknown

Supernatural horror is often used uncertainly to create fear. This allows filmmakers to build suspense by slowly revealing information about the supernatural threat.
