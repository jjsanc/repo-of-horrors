### Halloween

The 1978 classic Slasher Horror film Halloween follows escaped mental patient Michael Myers as he returns to his hometown to stalk a teenage babysitter.

##### Why Is It Famous

Halloween (1978) is famous because it popularized the modern slasher movie and set a new standard for independent horror filmmaking.

Instead of relying on blood and jump scares, the movie builds primal terror through patient storytelling.

#### Michael Myers

In John Carpenter's original vision, Michael has no rational motive, justification, or deep explanation. He represents the abstract embodiment of pure evil. 

#### Horror Techniques

- The unknown - Michael's lack of explanation makes him more frightening.
- Point-of-view shots - the audience sometimes sees events from Michael's perspective.
